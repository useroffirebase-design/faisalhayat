About Us

Built by gamers,
engineered for players

Faisal Hayat Gaming started as a small team of modders and competitive players who believed games should feel alive. Today we're an independent studio shipping original titles, running esports events, and building tools that help creators launch faster.

Every world we build starts with one question: is this fun? Everything else — the engine, the art, the netcode — exists to serve that.

  • Original IPs across PC, console and mobile
  • In-house esports & tournament operations
  • Player-first updates driven by community feedback
